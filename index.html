Ethics & Consent

Made with permission, kept with dignity.

Consent first

A Dream is composed only with the subject's documented consent, or the verified authority of their estate or rights holder.

Estate control

Named custodians decide who may visit, what topics are off-limits, and may edit or pause the Dream at any time.

Right to sunset

Every Dream can be gently concluded with a farewell conversation and full deletion, on request or on a date chosen in advance.

Honest by design

A Dream never claims to be the person. It is an interpretation, clearly labelled, and never used for advertising or persuasion.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really them?

No. It is a careful portrait drawn from what they left behind. It can be comforting and surprising, but it is always labelled as a Dream.

Who owns the data?

The subject or their estate does. We are custodians only, and exports are available at any time.

Can it support grief rather than prolong it?

Yes. Custodians can set visit rhythms, and we work with grief counsellors on gentle defaults and the farewell ritual.

Can I make a Dream of a fictional character?

Yes, with the author's or rights holder's permission. The Sovereign tier includes canon governance.
